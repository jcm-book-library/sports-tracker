## What to build

The `race` shape: `apps/race/index.html`, one file like the other apps. The owner already has ideas, so **start by asking for them**, then offer options with a recommendation before building. Things to settle with them:

- **Entering a race:** a card per race (Details fold for the extras), with the drivers of that season's lineup in finishing order, qualifying position, status, pole and fastest lap. Think about how to make entering twenty-two drivers quick on a phone.
- **Standings:** drivers' and teams' (and manufacturers', once powertrains are filled in), worked out from `Results`, `Points` and `Seasons`. Mid-season driver swaps come from `Lineups`.
- **Stats worth discussing:** wins, podiums, poles, fastest laps, points finishes, DNFs, form guide, qualifying v race position, team-mate head-to-head, circuit records, and season-by-season history using each season's own rules.
- **History:** loading 3,500+ rows is fine now, but think about loading old seasons on demand rather than all at once.
- **Hub:** set `href: 'formula-e/'` on the Formula E card in `apps/hub/index.html` and add the app to `tools/build_pages.py`, with its own manifest.
- **Reuse:** the standings maths, symmetrical layout, tests with a stubbed sheet, logos from Drive, and the paste-with-preview pattern (from the A-League scorers) are all in `apps/team-match` and `apps/tournament`.
